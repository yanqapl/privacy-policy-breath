Asthma Track

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Welcome to Asthma Track, an application designed to demonstrate simple asthma symptom tracking and respiratory health awareness.

Privacy matters to me. This page explains the purpose of the current demo version and what you should know before using it.

Demo mode

Asthma Track is currently provided in demo mode for demonstration and evaluation purposes. It lets you explore features such as symptom logging, daily check-ins, symptom history, and an illustrative risk score.

The demo does not require you to create an account. Please do not enter real personal information or sensitive medical information while trying it out. Use fictional or sample information instead.

Information you enter

If you interact with the demo, you may be able to enter symptom details, inhaler-use events, daily check-in answers, and notes. These inputs are intended only to demonstrate the application's interface and functionality.

Asthma Track is not intended to collect real patient medical records in its current demo version. If the application later introduces features that process personal or health information, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain those practices.

Third-party services

The demo may show illustrative environmental information, such as air quality or weather conditions. Such information should not be relied upon as real-time medical or environmental guidance unless expressly identified as live data.

Medical disclaimer

Asthma Track is not a medical device. Any risk score, suggested interpretation, or other health-related output in demo mode is illustrative only. It is not a diagnosis, medical advice, or a validated prediction of an asthma attack.

Do not use the demo to make treatment decisions. If you have concerns about your breathing or health, contact a qualified healthcare professional. In an emergency, seek immediate medical assistance.

Updates to this policy
