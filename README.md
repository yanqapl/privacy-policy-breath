# Asthma Track: Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 16, 2026**

Welcome to **Asthma Track**!

Asthma Track is an application created by an independent developer to demonstrate how people with asthma could log symptoms, complete daily check-ins, view their symptom history, and see an estimated asthma risk score.

As an independent developer, I take privacy seriously, particularly when an app relates to personal health.

## Current demo version

Asthma Track currently operates in **demo mode**. Its features are intended for demonstration and testing, rather than for managing real medical records.

The demo may display sample symptom entries, daily check-in results, environmental information, and an example risk score. Any information you enter into the demo is used to demonstrate the application's features.

**Please do not enter real personal information or sensitive health information into the demo.**

The current demo does not require you to create an account or provide your name or email address. I have not included advertising or third-party marketing trackers in the app.

## Medical disclaimer

Asthma Track is **not a medical device** and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Any risk score shown in the demo is illustrative and must not be used to make decisions about medication or medical care.

If you are experiencing breathing difficulties or another medical emergency, seek professional medical assistance immediately.

## Children's privacy

The demo is not intended for children to use independently. Please do not enter information about children into the app.

This policy may be updated if the app's features or privacy practices change. The date at the top will show when the policy was last revised.
